The face is the thing you look at most. Ours read like instruments: big numbers, honest gauges, colours you choose on the watch, and every pixel captured from the watch itself.

ATRAKTOS · every reading is live

  • Hours and minutes on large flip cards, with a separate seconds card (follows your watch's 12/24-hour setting)
  • Heart rate, step count and battery, each on its own live gauge ring
  • Weekday, date, weather condition and temperature
  • Calories and distance, calculated from your step count
  • Progress toward your daily step goal, in percent
