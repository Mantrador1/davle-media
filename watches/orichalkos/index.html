ORICHALKOS

ORICHALKOS is named after the mountain copper of Greek legend. Bronze analog hands sweep over a dark, layered dial beside a digital octagon.

This installs on your watch, not your phone.

It needs a round watch with Wear OS 5 or later.

  • Wear OS 5 or later
  • Round watches
  • Galaxy Watch4 and newer
  • Pixel Watch
Coming soon to Google PlayFollow DAVLE on Instagram for the release

What's on the dial

  1. Analog hours and minutes, with a sweeping seconds hand in the accent color
  2. Digital octagon: hours and minutes in large digits, following your watch's 12- or 24-hour setting, plus weekday, day and month
  3. Heart rate and step count, each with its own gauge ring
  4. Weather from your watch's own weather service: condition, temperature, day high and low, and a rain-chance ring
  5. Battery level on a segmented ring
  6. Moon phase
  7. Distance, estimated from your step count
  8. One small icon complication slot, empty by default
  9. Tap shortcuts: steps open your health app; the weather dial or the moon opens your weather app (on watches that include them)

7 colours, one setting

7 color themes: Oltremare, Smaragdi, Rubino, Melanzana, Petrolio, Noce and Nero. One setting recolors the rim markers, the four gauge rings and the seconds hand together, and tints the dial plate in every theme except Nero. Change it on the watch: touch and hold the face, then Customize.

When you lower your wrist

Always-on mode has its own quiet layout: the hour markers and the two main hands.
