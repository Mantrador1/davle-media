PYRSOS

PYRSOS is a hybrid instrument face: analog hands sweep over a dark technical dial, with a digital time window beside them.

This installs on your watch, not your phone.

It needs a round watch with Wear OS 5 or later.

  • Wear OS 5 or later
  • Round watches
  • Galaxy Watch4 and newer
  • Pixel Watch
Coming soon to Google PlayFollow DAVLE on Instagram for the release

What's on the dial

  1. Analog hours, minutes and a smoothly sweeping second hand
  2. Digital time window
  3. Battery level
  4. Heart rate, read from the watch sensor
  5. Step count
  6. Weekday and date
  7. Moon phase
  8. Weather condition and temperature, whenever your watch provides weather data
  9. Unread notification count

Tap shortcuts

  • PHONE opens the phone app
  • MUSIC opens the music player
  • MAIL opens Gmail
  • WEATHER opens Samsung Weather

7 colours, one setting

7 color themes to choose from. Change it on the watch: touch and hold the face, then Customize.
