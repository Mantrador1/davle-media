How it gets onto your watch

  1. Check your watch. Round, Wear OS 5 or later: Galaxy Watch4 and newer, Pixel Watch.
  2. Buy where your watch is. On the watch's Google Play, on a computer, or on a phone that shows an arrow next to the price.
  3. Pick it on the watch. Touch and hold your current face, swipe, and choose the new one.
