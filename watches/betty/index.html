BETTY

BETTY is a vintage hangar face: a weathered metal panel with an illustrated 1940s mechanic, analog hands over a large digital time, and your daily numbers on riveted plates.

This installs on your watch, not your phone.

It needs a round watch with Wear OS 5 or later.

  • Wear OS 5 or later
  • Round watches
  • Galaxy Watch4 and newer
  • Pixel Watch
Coming soon to Google PlayFollow DAVLE on Instagram for the release

Captured from the watch at 1:1

Every reading is live.

  • Analog hour, minute and seconds hands
  • Digital time with seconds
  • Date: weekday, day and month
  • Steps, with a progress bar
  • Battery level as a percentage and a segment bar
  • Weather: icon, temperature, high and low
  • Distance in km, estimated from your steps

When you lower your wrist.

A dedicated always-on layout shows the time and the date on a black dial with fine hour marks and the DAVLE mark.

Getting it onto your watch

BETTY is a watch face, so it lives on the watch. It is not on Google Play yet; these are the steps for when it is.

On your phone, watch paired

  1. Open BETTY in the Google Play app.
  2. If you see an arrow next to the price, tap it and choose your watch.
  3. Buy. Keep the watch near the phone; it can take a few minutes to arrive.
  4. On the watch, touch and hold the current face, swipe, and choose DAVLE BETTY.

No arrow? Your phone does not see a compatible watch on this Google account. Don't buy on the phone yet: use the watch or a computer instead.

On your watch

  1. Open Google Play on the watch.
  2. Search for DAVLE BETTY and install it.
  3. Touch and hold the current face, swipe to the end, and add BETTY.

On Galaxy watches you can also touch and hold the face and swipe left to More watch faces.

On a computer

  1. Open the BETTY page on Google Play once it is released, signed in with the same Google account as your watch.
  2. Choose your watch in the device list, then install.
  3. Add the face on the watch: touch and hold, swipe, choose BETTY.
It's installed but not in my watch face list

Give it a few minutes and keep the watch on Wi-Fi or near the phone. Then touch and hold the current face and swipe all the way to the end. If it still isn't there, restart the watch. Still missing? Write to us with your watch model.

Google Play says it isn't compatible

BETTY needs a round watch on Wear OS 5 or later. Update the watch software first. Galaxy Watch4, Watch5 and Watch6 need the One UI 6 Watch update. Square watches can't run it.

Customizing in the Galaxy Wearable app doesn't work

Customize on the watch instead: touch and hold the face, then Customize. It is quicker and always shows every option.

Questions

Will it work on my phone?

No. BETTY is a watch face for Wear OS watches. Your phone can only help you install it.

Which watches can run it?

Round watches with Wear OS 5 or later: Galaxy Watch4 and newer (Watch4 to Watch6 need One UI 6 Watch), Galaxy Watch Ultra, Pixel Watch, and other round Wear OS 5 watches. Not Apple Watch, not Fitbit, not square watches.

How long does it take to reach the watch?

Usually a few minutes after buying on the phone. Keep the watch connected.

Why does heart rate or steps show nothing?

The watch face reads the watch's own sensors. Allow the permissions when the watch asks, and wear the watch for a moment so it can take a reading.

Can I get a refund?

Google Play lets you ask for a refund within 48 hours of buying, from your order history. After that, write to us with your watch model and order number and we'll help.

How do I contact you?

Email studio@davlestudio.com. Include your watch model, so we can answer in one go.
