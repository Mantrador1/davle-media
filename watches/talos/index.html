TALOS

TALOS reads like a riveted metal instrument panel, not a flat dial. Analog hour and minute hands sweep wide near the centre and thin out over the readings beneath them, with a slim second hand alongside.

This installs on your watch, not your phone.

It needs a round watch with Wear OS 5 or later.

  • Wear OS 5 or later
  • Round watches
  • Galaxy Watch4 and newer
  • Pixel Watch
Coming soon to Google PlayFollow DAVLE on Instagram for the release

What's on the dial

  1. Analog hour, minute and second hands over a blackened-metal, screw-plated dial
  2. A digital time module on the left with hours, minutes and seconds in 12-hour or 24-hour format, following your watch setting, with AM/PM in 12-hour format, plus a week/day/year line underneath
  3. A weekday arc with today marked in red, and a date circle
  4. Four data tiles you can set: the defaults are step count, sunrise/sunset, heart rate and your next calendar event
  5. Weather condition, current temperature and the day's high and low
  6. A GOAL arc: a row of cells that fills with your step percentage, with a red pointer on the same scale
  7. A battery gauge marked E to F, with a live percentage readout
  8. A moon phase disc
  9. Phone, messages and music buttons; the messages button shows your unread count

7 colours, one setting

Seven clearly different colourways on the same dial: Original, MA-1 orange, Zinc Yellow, Marine, Turquoise, Phosphor green and Blue Lilac. The digital display, the gauges, the hands and the data texts take the colour you pick. Change it on the watch: touch and hold the face, then Customize.

When you lower your wrist

A sparse always-on view keeps the date, the digital time, and outlines of the hands and the minute ring visible at low power.
