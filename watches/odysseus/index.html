World-map dial, 7 colors, battery, heart-rate and step instruments.

Coming soon. DAVLE ODYSSEUS is not on Google Play yet, so there is no purchase link. To ask when it arrives: studio@davlestudio.com.

What you need

  • A round Wear OS watch running Wear OS 5 or newer (Android 14 based, API level 34).
  • A Google Account signed in on the watch, and a way to pay in Google Play.
  • Wi-Fi or LTE on the watch while it downloads.
  • A phone is handy for buying and for choosing the face, but is not required: you can also buy from the Play Store on the watch.

This is a watch face, not a phone app. It installs on the watch. A small helper may appear on the phone as a shortcut to the watch install; the watch face works without it.

How to buy and install

Not on Google Play yet. These are the steps for when it is.

Pick one route. The watch needs Wi-Fi or LTE, and the watch and your purchase should use the same Google Account.

Route 1: from your phone

  1. Open Google Play on your phone and go to DAVLE ODYSSEUS.
  2. Tap the arrow next to the price or Install button and choose your watch as the device.
  3. Buy it, or tap Install if you already own it. It can take a few minutes to reach the watch.

Route 2: from the watch

  1. Open the watch's app list and tap Play Store. It uses the Google Account you set the watch up with.
  2. Tap Search and type or say "DAVLE ODYSSEUS".
  3. Open the result, then buy it or tap Install.

Route 3: from Google Play on a computer

  1. Open the DAVLE ODYSSEUS page on play.google.com and sign in.
  2. Click the price or Install button. Where it says "Choose a device", pick your Wear OS watch.

If your watch is not offered as a device, it probably does not meet the Wear OS 5 requirement.

Put it on your watch screen

Installing a watch face does not switch to it. You also select it.

On the watch

  1. Touch and hold the current watch face. On some watches, swipe left on the watch screen first.
  2. Swipe left or right until you see DAVLE ODYSSEUS, then tap it.

From your phone

  • Samsung Galaxy Watch: open the Galaxy Wearable app, tap Watch faces, then tap DAVLE ODYSSEUS.
  • Pixel Watch: open the Pixel Watch app, tap Watch faces, then Use on watch under DAVLE ODYSSEUS.
  • Other Wear OS watches: use the Wear OS app or your watch maker's app.

Steps can differ by model and software version. A watch face can be hidden, and a hidden face still shows in the phone app.

Refunds

Google Play handles refunds. For apps you may be able to get one if you ask within 48 hours of buying, depending on the purchase; after that Google asks you to contact the developer. See Request a refund on Google Play.
