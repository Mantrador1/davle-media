TACHOS

TACHOS is a digital instrument face: a large time readout in the center, with two analog gauges beside it. A red needle shows the battery level on the upper gauge and your heart rate on the lower one.

This installs on your watch, not your phone.

It needs a round watch with Wear OS 5 or later.

  • Wear OS 5 or later
  • Round watches
  • Galaxy Watch4 and newer
  • Pixel Watch
Coming soon to Google PlayFollow DAVLE on Instagram for the release

What's on the dial

  1. Digital time with seconds, in 12-hour or 24-hour format, following your watch setting
  2. Weekday, day and month
  3. Battery level on an analog gauge marked PWR %
  4. Heart rate on an analog gauge, with the BPM number at its hub
  5. Step count with a progress bar for your step goal
  6. Weather condition and temperature
  7. Daily high and low temperature
  8. Chance of rain
  9. Next calendar event
  10. Unread notification count
  11. Sunrise or sunset time

7 colours, one setting

7 color themes to choose from. Each theme recolors the two gauge dials and the accent lights together. Change it on the watch: touch and hold the face, then Customize.

When you lower your wrist

A separate, minimal always-on layout with the time and the date.
